# Employment contract

**Document ID:** EMP-2961
**Dated:** 15 November 2025

Kai Glendale has entered into an employment agreement with Rayford Oilfield Services Ltd to serve as a petroleum engineer. Under the terms of the contract, compensation is distributed on a monthly basis in US dollars and deposited directly into a local bank account.

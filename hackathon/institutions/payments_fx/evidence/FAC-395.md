# Invoice finance agreement with Calwick Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-395
**Dated:** 24 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Calwick Invoice Finance Ltd provides an advance to Calfield Fine Foods Ltd equal to 85% of the total value of each invoice approved by them. These funds are deposited directly into the client's account held at the payments firm. Furthermore, Calwick Invoice Finance Ltd is responsible for the direct collection of all invoices covered by this arrangement.

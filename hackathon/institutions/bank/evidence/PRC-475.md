# Cash handling procedure, Whitridge Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-475
**Dated:** 9 July 2024

Wilridge Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kingscote Market Square branch.

Elmstead Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kingscote Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

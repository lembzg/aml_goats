# Cash handling procedure, Marwick Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-7899
**Dated:** 15 July 2024

Everwell Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Radmere Market Square branch.

Cranton Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Radmere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Cash handling procedure, Ashcote Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-4542
**Dated:** 19 June 2024

Falmore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Talholt Market Square branch.

Aldden Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Talholt Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

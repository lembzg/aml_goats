# Invoice finance agreement with Aldgate Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-4766
**Dated:** 2 March 2026

Under this facility, Aldgate Invoice Finance Ltd provides Stanford Timber Supplies Ltd with an advance of 85% against the total value of each approved customer invoice. These funds are disbursed directly to the client's designated account held with the payments firm, while Aldgate Invoice Finance Ltd retains responsibility for the direct collection of all invoices.

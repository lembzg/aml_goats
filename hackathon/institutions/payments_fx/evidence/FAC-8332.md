# Invoice finance agreement with Corley Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-8332
**Dated:** 26 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement with Corley Invoice Finance Ltd, Thornley Fine Foods Ltd will receive an advance of 85% on the value of each approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, with Corley Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of all invoices.

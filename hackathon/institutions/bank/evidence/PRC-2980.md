# Cash handling procedure, Brackley Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2980
**Dated:** 25 April 2025

Talfield Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Stanwick Market Square branch.

Kelton Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Stanwick Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Invoice finance agreement with Ferndale Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-2980
**Dated:** 23 January 2026

Under this agreement, Ferndale Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Hartford Apparel Ltd with an advance of 85% on the value of all approved customer invoices. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s designated account held with the payments firm, and Ferndale Invoice Finance Ltd will retain responsibility for the direct collection of these invoices.

# Supply contract with Rayford Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-3533
**Dated:** 3 July 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Rayford Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to provide dried apricots, figs, and dates to Cranton Apparel Ltd. The arrangement stipulates that deliveries will occur on a quarterly basis over a 12-month term. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon shipment.

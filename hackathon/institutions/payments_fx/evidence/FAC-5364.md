# Invoice finance agreement with Hollmore Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-5364
**Dated:** 22 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Hollmore Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Langcombe Electronics Ltd with an advance of 85% on the total value of all approved customer invoices. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s account held at the payments firm. Additionally, Hollmore Invoice Finance Ltd assumes responsibility for the direct collection of all invoices covered under this facility.

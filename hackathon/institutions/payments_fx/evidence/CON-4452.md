# Supply contract with Stancote Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-4452
**Dated:** 7 September 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Stancote Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will act as the supplier for Lyndale Fresh Produce Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, the supplier is to provide dried apricots, figs, and dates. 

Deliveries are scheduled to be fulfilled in quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and the agreed-upon payment terms stipulate that settlement is due upon shipment.

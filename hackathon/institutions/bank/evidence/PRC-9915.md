# Cash handling procedure, Drayden Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-9915
**Dated:** 2 September 2024

Lockwick Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calmore Market Square branch.

Raydale Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calmore Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

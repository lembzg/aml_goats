# Cash handling procedure, Marholt Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5108
**Dated:** 27 September 2024

Elmley Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Falwell Market Square branch.

Calgate Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Falwell Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Cash handling procedure, Marcote Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-7816
**Dated:** 24 August 2024

Oakwell Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Crancombe Market Square branch.

Bramholt Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Crancombe Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

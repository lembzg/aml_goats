# Invoice finance agreement with Bramgate Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-6518
**Dated:** 22 February 2026

Under the terms of this facility, Bramgate Invoice Finance Ltd is committed to providing an advance of 85% on the total value of all customer invoices approved for funding. These funds will be disbursed directly into the account held by Penfield Engineering Supplies Ltd at the payments firm. Additionally, Bramgate Invoice Finance Ltd will assume responsibility for the direct collection of the assigned invoices.

# Supply contract with Wilcombe Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-6922
**Dated:** 29 August 2026

This supply agreement formalizes the arrangement between the overseas supplier, Wilcombe Dried Fruits Co of Erbil, and the UK-based importer, Elmcombe Catering Supplies Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, Wilcombe Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. The delivery schedule is set for quarterly intervals over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and the contractual obligation requires payment to be settled upon shipment.

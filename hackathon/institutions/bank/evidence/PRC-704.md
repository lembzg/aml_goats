# Cash handling procedure, Selcote Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-704
**Dated:** 17 March 2024

Wilby Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Wildale Market Square branch.

Standale Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Wildale Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

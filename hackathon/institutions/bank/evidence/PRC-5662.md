# Cash handling procedure, Langstead Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5662
**Dated:** 20 July 2024

Millmere Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Everden Market Square branch.

Penstead Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Everden Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

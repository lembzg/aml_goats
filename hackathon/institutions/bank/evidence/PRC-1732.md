# Cash handling procedure, Corstead Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1732
**Dated:** 21 February 2025

Corgate Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calden Market Square branch.

Wenworth Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calden Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

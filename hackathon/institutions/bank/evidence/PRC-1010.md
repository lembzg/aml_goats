# Cash handling procedure, Thorngate Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1010
**Dated:** 23 August 2024

Dunmere Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calridge Market Square branch.

Ashcote Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calridge Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

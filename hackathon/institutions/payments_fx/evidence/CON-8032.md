# Supply contract with Glendale Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-8032
**Dated:** 8 September 2026

This agreement outlines the supply arrangement between Talley Electronics Ltd and Glendale Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil. Under the terms of the contract, Glendale Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. These deliveries are scheduled on a quarterly basis over a 12-month period. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment required upon the shipment of goods.

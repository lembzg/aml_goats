# Supply contract with Glenhurst Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9012
**Dated:** 2 September 2026

This agreement confirms that Glenhurst Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will act as the supplier for Whitworth Timber Supplies Ltd. The contract covers the provision of dried apricots, figs, and dates, which are to be delivered in quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is set in US dollars, and payment obligations are triggered upon each shipment.

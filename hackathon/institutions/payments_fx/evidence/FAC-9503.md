# Invoice finance agreement with Crandale Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-9503
**Dated:** 19 January 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Crandale Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Marridge Fresh Produce Ltd with an advance of 85% against the total value of each approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, with Crandale Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of the underlying invoices.

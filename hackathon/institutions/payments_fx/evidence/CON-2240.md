# Supply contract with Kingsden Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2240
**Dated:** 5 July 2026

This agreement confirms that Kingsden Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will act as the supplier of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Kelgate Timber Supplies Ltd. The term of the supply arrangement is set for 12 months, with deliveries scheduled to occur in quarterly shipments. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and the terms stipulate that payment must be made upon shipment.

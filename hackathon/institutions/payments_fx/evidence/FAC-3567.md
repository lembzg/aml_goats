# Invoice finance agreement with Lockstead Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-3567
**Dated:** 29 March 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Lockstead Invoice Finance Ltd is set to provide Thornton Catering Supplies Ltd with an advance of 85% against the value of all approved customer invoices. These funds will be remitted directly to the client's designated account held at the payments firm, with Lockstead Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of the invoices.

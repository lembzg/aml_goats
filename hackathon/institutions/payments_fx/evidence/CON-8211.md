# Supply contract with Draymere Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-8211
**Dated:** 2 July 2026

This supply agreement establishes that Draymere Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Drayridge Catering Supplies Ltd. The contract term is set for 12 months, with all pricing fixed in US dollars. Settlement of invoices is required upon shipment.

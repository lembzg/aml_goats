# Cash handling procedure, Wilmore Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-3557
**Dated:** 10 November 2024

Standale Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millford Market Square branch.

Talmere Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millford Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

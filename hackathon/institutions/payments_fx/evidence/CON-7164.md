# Supply contract with Selgate Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-7164
**Dated:** 6 August 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Selgate Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Hartden Catering Supplies Ltd. The contract term is set for 12 months. All pricing is established in US dollars, and payment obligations are triggered upon shipment.

# Cash handling procedure, Bramley Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-9397
**Dated:** 14 August 2024

Selmore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Garden Market Square branch.

Draystead Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Garden Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

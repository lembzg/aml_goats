# Supply contract with Wenmore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-3999
**Dated:** 2 September 2026

This agreement confirms the supply arrangement between Wenmore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Fernmore Fresh Produce Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, Wenmore Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Deliveries are scheduled on a quarterly basis over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, with payment obligations becoming due upon shipment.

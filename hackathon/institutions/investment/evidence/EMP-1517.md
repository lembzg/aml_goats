# Employment contract

**Document ID:** EMP-1517
**Dated:** 17 November 2025

Thornmere Oilfield Services Ltd has engaged Sam Elmby to serve as a petroleum engineer at its Basra location. This engagement is formalized through a two-year employment contract. Under the terms of this agreement, compensation is disbursed on a monthly basis in US dollars, with funds deposited directly into a local bank account.

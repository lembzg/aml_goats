# Cash handling procedure, Pengate Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5980
**Dated:** 20 May 2024

Normore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Marhurst Market Square branch.

Oakden Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Marhurst Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

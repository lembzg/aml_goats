# Invoice finance agreement with Kelstead Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-5372
**Dated:** 20 February 2026

Under this facility, Kelstead Invoice Finance Ltd provides an advance of 85% against the total value of all approved customer invoices submitted by Raycombe Catering Supplies Ltd. Disbursements are issued directly into the client's designated account held with the payments firm, and Kelstead Invoice Finance Ltd retains responsibility for the direct collection of these invoices.

# Cash handling procedure, Hollwell Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-9934
**Dated:** 4 March 2025

Calwick Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Falton Market Square branch.

Aldmere Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Falton Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

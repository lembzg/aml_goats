# Cash handling procedure, Calford Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-4546
**Dated:** 7 May 2024

Talfield Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Oakhurst Market Square branch.

Glenholt Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Oakhurst Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

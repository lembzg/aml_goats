# Supply contract with Penmore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-1057
**Dated:** 29 June 2026

This agreement confirms that Penmore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will act as the supplier of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Elmmore Fine Foods Ltd. The contract mandates quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon shipment.

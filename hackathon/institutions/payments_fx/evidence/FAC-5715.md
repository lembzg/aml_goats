# Invoice finance agreement with Hartworth Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-5715
**Dated:** 20 March 2026

Under the terms of this facility, Hartworth Invoice Finance Ltd agrees to provide Wenmere Fine Foods Ltd with an advance of 85% of the total value for every approved customer invoice. These funds are to be deposited directly into the client's account held at the payments firm. Furthermore, Hartworth Invoice Finance Ltd will retain responsibility for the direct collection of all assigned invoices.

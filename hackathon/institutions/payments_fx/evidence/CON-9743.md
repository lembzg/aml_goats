# Supply contract with Hollwell Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9743
**Dated:** 30 July 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Hollwell Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to provide Raygate Wine Merchants Ltd with regular consignments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. The supply schedule is set for quarterly shipments over a 12-month duration. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and the terms stipulate that payment must be settled upon shipment.

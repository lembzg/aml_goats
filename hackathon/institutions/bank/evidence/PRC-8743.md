# Cash handling procedure, Glenmore Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-8743
**Dated:** 7 November 2024

Selden Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calcote Market Square branch.

Everton Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Calcote Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Cash handling procedure, Elmcote Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-7182
**Dated:** 28 September 2024

Garhurst Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Sandfield Market Square branch.

Wenley Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Sandfield Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

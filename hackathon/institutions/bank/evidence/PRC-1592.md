# Cash handling procedure, Norley Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1592
**Dated:** 17 March 2025

Kelmore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Oakmere Market Square branch.

Dunby Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Oakmere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Supply contract with Wenstead Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9371
**Dated:** 7 July 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Wenstead Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to provide a consistent supply of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Penley Engineering Supplies Ltd. 

The arrangement stipulates that deliveries will occur in quarterly shipments over a total duration of 12 months. All pricing for these goods is fixed in US dollars, and payment is required in full upon shipment.

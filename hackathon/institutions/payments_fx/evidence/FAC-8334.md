# Invoice finance agreement with Penwell Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-8334
**Dated:** 23 February 2026

Under the terms of this agreement, Penwell Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Bramdale Timber Supplies Ltd with an advance of 85% of the total value for each invoice approved. These funds are to be remitted directly into the client's account held at the payments firm, and Penwell Invoice Finance Ltd retains the responsibility for the direct collection of all such invoices.

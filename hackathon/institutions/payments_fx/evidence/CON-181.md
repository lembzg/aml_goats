# Supply contract with Raycote Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-181
**Dated:** 11 July 2026

This agreement confirms that Raycote Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to supply a variety of dried fruits, specifically apricots, figs, and dates, to Garden Fine Foods Ltd. The arrangement stipulates that deliveries will occur in quarterly shipments over a 12-month duration. All pricing is set in US dollars, and the terms require payment to be settled upon shipment.

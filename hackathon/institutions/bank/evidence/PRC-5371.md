# Cash handling procedure, Draywell Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5371
**Dated:** 31 August 2024

Hollmore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millby Market Square branch.

Millford Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millby Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

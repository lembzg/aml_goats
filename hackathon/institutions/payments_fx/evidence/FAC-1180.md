# Invoice finance agreement with Wilholt Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-1180
**Dated:** 22 March 2026

Under this agreement, Wilholt Invoice Finance Ltd provides a facility to Lockfield Apparel Ltd whereby 85% of the value of every approved customer invoice is advanced to the client's account held at the payments firm. Wilholt Invoice Finance Ltd is responsible for the direct collection of these invoices.

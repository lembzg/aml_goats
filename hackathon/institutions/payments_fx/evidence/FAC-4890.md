# Invoice finance agreement with Sandden Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-4890
**Dated:** 26 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Sandden Invoice Finance Ltd shall provide Langfield Catering Supplies Ltd with an advance of 85% against the total value of each approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, with Sandden Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of all such invoices.

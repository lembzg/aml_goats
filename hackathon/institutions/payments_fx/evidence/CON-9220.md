# Supply contract with Sandmore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9220
**Dated:** 9 September 2026

This contract confirms that Sandmore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has entered into an agreement to supply dried apricots, figs, and dates to Halbrook Furniture Imports Ltd. The supply arrangement consists of quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment is required to be made upon shipment.

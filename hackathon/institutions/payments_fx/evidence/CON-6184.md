# Supply contract with Normore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-6184
**Dated:** 5 August 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Normore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide dried apricots, figs, and dates to Everridge Wine Merchants Ltd. The contract stipulates a 12-month term, with inventory delivered in quarterly shipments. All pricing is set in US dollars, and payment obligations for each delivery are triggered upon shipment.

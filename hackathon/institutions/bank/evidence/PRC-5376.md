# Cash handling procedure, Lynholt Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5376
**Dated:** 31 October 2024

Norton Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitwell Market Square branch.

Thornhurst Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitwell Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

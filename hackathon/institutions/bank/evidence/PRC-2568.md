# Cash handling procedure, Nordale Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2568
**Dated:** 9 May 2025

Kelmere Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Penworth Market Square branch.

Ferndale Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Penworth Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

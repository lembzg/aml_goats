# Invoice finance agreement with Raywell Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-719
**Dated:** 1 March 2026

Under the terms of this agreement, Raywell Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Oakgate Electronics Ltd with an advance of 85% on the total value of all approved customer invoices. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s account held at the payments firm, with Raywell Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of the invoiced amounts.

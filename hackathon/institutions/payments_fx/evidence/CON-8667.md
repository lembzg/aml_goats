# Supply contract with Millridge Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-8667
**Dated:** 12 July 2026

This agreement formalizes the supply arrangement between Millridge Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Brommore Fresh Produce Ltd. Under the terms of this contract, Millridge Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Deliveries are scheduled on a quarterly basis for a duration of 12 months. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations becoming due upon shipment.

# Supply contract with Everwell Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-7986
**Dated:** 2 August 2026

This supply agreement formalizes the arrangement between Everwell Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Aldmore Apparel Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, Everwell Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. These deliveries are scheduled to occur on a quarterly basis over a 12-month period. All pricing is set in US dollars, and payment is required at the time of shipment.

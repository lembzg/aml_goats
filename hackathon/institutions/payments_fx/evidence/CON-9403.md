# Supply contract with Halbrook Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9403
**Dated:** 10 August 2026

This supply agreement is entered into between Hartstead Furniture Imports Ltd and Halbrook Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil. Under the terms of this contract, Halbrook Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. The supply schedule is set for quarterly deliveries over a 12-month duration. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment obligations for each shipment are due at the time of dispatch.

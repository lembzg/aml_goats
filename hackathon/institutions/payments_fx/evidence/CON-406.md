# Supply contract with Raymore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-406
**Dated:** 29 August 2026

This agreement formalizes the supply arrangement between Raymore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Oakmore Wine Merchants Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, Raymore Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Deliveries are scheduled to occur on a quarterly basis over a total duration of 12 months. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment for each consignment is required upon shipment.

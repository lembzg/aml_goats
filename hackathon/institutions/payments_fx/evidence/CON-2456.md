# Supply contract with Evercombe Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2456
**Dated:** 1 August 2026

This agreement confirms that Evercombe Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to provide supplies of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Drayton Fresh Produce Ltd. The delivery schedule is set for quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon shipment.

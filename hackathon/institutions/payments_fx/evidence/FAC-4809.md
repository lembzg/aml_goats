# Invoice finance agreement with Garmere Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-4809
**Dated:** 23 January 2026

Under the terms of this agreement, Garmere Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Corcombe Wine Merchants Ltd with an advance of 85% against the total value of all approved customer invoices. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s designated account held with the payments firm, and Garmere Invoice Finance Ltd retains the responsibility for the direct collection of the invoices.

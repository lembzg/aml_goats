# Supply contract with Kingsgate Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-981
**Dated:** 6 August 2026

This agreement outlines the terms for the supply of dried apricots, figs, and dates by Kingsgate Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, to Penworth Electronics Ltd. Under the terms of this contract, goods are to be delivered in quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations becoming due upon shipment.

# Supply contract with Drayley Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2650
**Dated:** 1 September 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Drayley Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Lockdale Fine Foods Ltd over a 12-month period. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations falling due upon shipment.

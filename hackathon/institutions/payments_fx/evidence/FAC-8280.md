# Invoice finance agreement with Marwell Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-8280
**Dated:** 2 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement with Marwell Invoice Finance Ltd, Bramstead Fine Foods Ltd will receive an advance of 85% of the total value for every approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, with Marwell Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of all invoices.

# Cash handling procedure, Millcombe Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2540
**Dated:** 2 July 2024

Langcombe Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kingsmere Market Square branch.

Standen Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kingsmere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Invoice finance agreement with Elmstead Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-1751
**Dated:** 20 March 2026

Under the terms of the agreement, Elmstead Invoice Finance Ltd shall provide Halford Timber Supplies Ltd with an advance of 85% of the total value for every approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, with Elmstead Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of all invoices.

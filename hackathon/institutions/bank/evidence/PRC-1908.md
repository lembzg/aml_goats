# Cash handling procedure, Drayby Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1908
**Dated:** 3 March 2025

Selmore Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitford Market Square branch.

Hollwick Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitford Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

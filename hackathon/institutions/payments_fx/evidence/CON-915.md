# Supply contract with Millgate Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-915
**Dated:** 7 July 2026

This supply agreement establishes that Millgate Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide Norworth Electronics Ltd with a regular supply of dried apricots, figs, and dates. The arrangement is set for a duration of 12 months, with product deliveries scheduled on a quarterly basis. All pricing is locked in US dollars, and Norworth Electronics Ltd is required to settle payments at the time of shipment.

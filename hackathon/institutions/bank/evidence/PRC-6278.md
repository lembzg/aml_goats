# Cash handling procedure, Falmere Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-6278
**Dated:** 7 May 2024

Brambrook Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Evermere Market Square branch.

Corcombe Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Evermere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

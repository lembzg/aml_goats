# Cash handling procedure, Everley Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2927
**Dated:** 23 June 2024

Bramfield Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Penton Market Square branch.

Rayfield Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Penton Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

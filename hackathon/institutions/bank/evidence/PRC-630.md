# Cash handling procedure, Elmstead Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-630
**Dated:** 17 September 2024

Bromton Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Bromton Market Square branch.

Cranden Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Bromton Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

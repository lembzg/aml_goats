# Invoice finance agreement with Corford Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-7196
**Dated:** 2 February 2026

Under the terms of this facility, Corford Invoice Finance Ltd provides an advance of 85% against the total value of each approved customer invoice submitted by Elmfield Fine Foods Ltd. Funds are disbursed directly to the client's account held at the payments firm, while Corford Invoice Finance Ltd retains responsibility for the direct collection of all assigned invoices.

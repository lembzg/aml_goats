# Supply contract with Langworth Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-6267
**Dated:** 10 August 2026

This supply agreement establishes that Langworth Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Wenley Wine Merchants Ltd over a 12-month period. All pricing is set in US dollars, with payment terms stipulated as due upon shipment.

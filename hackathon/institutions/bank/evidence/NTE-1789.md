# Note by Dale, Wenden Retail Park branch

**Document ID:** NTE-1789
**Dated:** 10 September 2026

Customer Max Wenmore attended the branch today to deposit £9,750.00 in cash into the account for Rayhurst Trading Ltd. 

During the transaction, the customer inquired about our aggregation policy, specifically asking if cash deposits made across different branches are cumulative. He also questioned whether keeping individual deposits below the £10,000 threshold would exempt the transaction from reporting requirements. I clarified that our internal monitoring procedures apply to cash deposits of any size. 

Mr. Wenmore appeared dissatisfied with this information and stated that he would use a different branch for future deposits.

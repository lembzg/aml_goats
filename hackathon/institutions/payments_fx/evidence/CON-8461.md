# Supply contract with Lynstead Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-8461
**Dated:** 2 August 2026

This agreement outlines the supply arrangement between Lynstead Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Talwick Furniture Imports Ltd. Under the terms of the contract, Lynstead Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Deliveries are scheduled on a quarterly basis for a duration of 12 months. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, with payment required upon shipment.

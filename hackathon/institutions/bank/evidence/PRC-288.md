# Cash handling procedure, Norcombe Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-288
**Dated:** 4 April 2024

Langley Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Selcote Market Square branch.

Raymore Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Selcote Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

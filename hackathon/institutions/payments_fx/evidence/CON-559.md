# Supply contract with Lockmore Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-559
**Dated:** 29 July 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Lockmore Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has entered into a contract to provide dried apricots, figs, and dates to Ferngate Catering Supplies Ltd. The arrangement stipulates that goods will be delivered in quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations falling due upon the shipment of each order.

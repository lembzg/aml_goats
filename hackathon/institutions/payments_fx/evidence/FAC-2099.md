# Invoice finance agreement with Thornby Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-2099
**Dated:** 1 February 2026

Under this facility, Thornby Invoice Finance Ltd agrees to provide Brackby Furniture Imports Ltd with an advance of 85% of the total value for every approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s designated account held at the payments firm, with Thornby Invoice Finance Ltd assuming responsibility for the direct collection of all such invoices.

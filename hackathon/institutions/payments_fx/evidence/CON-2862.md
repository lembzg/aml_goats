# Supply contract with Oakhurst Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2862
**Dated:** 6 August 2026

This agreement confirms that Oakhurst Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will serve as the supplier of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Brackden Fine Foods Ltd. The contract mandates quarterly shipments over a 12-month duration. All pricing is set in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon the shipment of goods.

# Cash handling procedure, Brommere Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-9971
**Dated:** 30 November 2024

Glengate Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitmere Market Square branch.

Marfield Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Whitmere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

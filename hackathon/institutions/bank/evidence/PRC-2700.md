# Cash handling procedure, Corwell Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2700
**Dated:** 2 October 2024

Elmhurst Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Brammore Market Square branch.

Langstead Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Brammore Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Supply contract with Wilridge Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-4784
**Dated:** 1 September 2026

This agreement formalizes a supply arrangement between Wilridge Dried Fruits Co of Erbil and Garford Furniture Imports Ltd. Under the terms of this contract, Wilridge Dried Fruits Co will provide shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. The schedule for these deliveries is set on a quarterly basis over a period of 12 months. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment is required upon shipment.

# Cash handling procedure, Wenmere Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1352
**Dated:** 17 August 2024

Cranwick Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Ashmere Market Square branch.

Elmholt Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Ashmere Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

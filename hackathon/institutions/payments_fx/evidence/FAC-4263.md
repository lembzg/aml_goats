# Invoice finance agreement with Lockridge Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-4263
**Dated:** 1 March 2026

Under the terms of this facility, Lockridge Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Bramcote Wine Merchants Ltd with an advance of 85% against the face value of all approved customer invoices. Funds are to be remitted directly into the client's account held at the payments firm. Furthermore, Lockridge Invoice Finance Ltd retains responsibility for the direct collection of these invoices.

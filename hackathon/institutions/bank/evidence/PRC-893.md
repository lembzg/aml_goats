# Cash handling procedure, Corcombe Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-893
**Dated:** 26 June 2024

Lockton Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Aldford Market Square branch.

Everfield Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Aldford Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

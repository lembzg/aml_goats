# Cash handling procedure, Calridge Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5781
**Dated:** 18 March 2025

Thornwell Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Fernbrook Market Square branch.

Dunwell Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Fernbrook Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Supply contract with Oakfield Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9439
**Dated:** 28 July 2026

This supply agreement confirms that Oakfield Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Elmridge Catering Supplies Ltd. The contract term is set for 12 months. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon the dispatch of each shipment.

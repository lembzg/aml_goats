# Cash handling procedure, Kelholt Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2281
**Dated:** 26 February 2025

Wenby Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kelwick Market Square branch.

Corhurst Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Kelwick Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

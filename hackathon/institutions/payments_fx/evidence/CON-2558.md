# Supply contract with Draystead Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2558
**Dated:** 4 August 2026

This agreement confirms that Draystead Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to provide supplies of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Thornford Furniture Imports Ltd. The arrangement stipulates a 12-month term, with deliveries to be fulfilled through quarterly shipments. All pricing is established in US dollars, and the terms require payment to be settled upon shipment.

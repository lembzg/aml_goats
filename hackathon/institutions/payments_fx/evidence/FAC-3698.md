# Invoice finance agreement with Langhurst Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-3698
**Dated:** 21 February 2026

Under the terms of the agreement with Langhurst Invoice Finance Ltd, Wilwick Wine Merchants Ltd will receive an advance of 85% of the total value for every approved customer invoice. These funds are to be remitted directly to the client’s designated account held with the payments firm, and Langhurst Invoice Finance Ltd will be responsible for the direct collection of all invoices.

# Supply contract with Halley Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-7539
**Dated:** 9 August 2026

This agreement confirms that Halley Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, has been contracted to supply dried apricots, figs, and dates to Millford Engineering Supplies Ltd. Deliveries are scheduled to occur in quarterly shipments over a 12-month period. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment obligations are triggered upon shipment.

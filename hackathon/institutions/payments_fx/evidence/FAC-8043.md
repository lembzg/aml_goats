# Invoice finance agreement with Talton Invoice Finance Ltd

**Document ID:** FAC-8043
**Dated:** 2 April 2026

Under this agreement, Talton Invoice Finance Ltd will provide Corgate Fine Foods Ltd with an advance of 85% of the value of every approved customer invoice. These funds will be disbursed directly into the client's account held at the payments firm, and Talton Invoice Finance Ltd will be responsible for the direct collection of all invoices.

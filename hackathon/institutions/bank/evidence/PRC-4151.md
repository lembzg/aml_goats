# Cash handling procedure, Halstead Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-4151
**Dated:** 1 April 2025

Falfield Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millridge Market Square branch.

Corstead Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Millridge Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Cash handling procedure, Wilwick Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-2577
**Dated:** 15 April 2024

Ashbrook Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Sandbrook Market Square branch.

Glenbrook Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Sandbrook Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

# Cash handling procedure, Lockcote Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-5715
**Dated:** 7 January 2025

Corfield Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Thorngate Market Square branch.

Kelmore Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Thorngate Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

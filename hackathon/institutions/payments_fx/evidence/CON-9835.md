# Supply contract with Bramfield Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-9835
**Dated:** 12 July 2026

This agreement outlines the terms between Bramfield Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, and Ferncote Fine Foods Ltd regarding the importation of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Under the terms of the contract, goods will be delivered in quarterly shipments over a period of 12 months. All pricing is established in US dollars, with payment obligations triggered upon the shipment of goods.

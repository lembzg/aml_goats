# Supply contract with Caldale Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-520
**Dated:** 3 September 2026

This supply agreement establishes that Caldale Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide quarterly shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates to Corden Catering Supplies Ltd. The duration of this contract is set for 12 months. All pricing is fixed in US dollars, and payment is required upon shipment.

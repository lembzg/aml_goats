# Cash handling procedure, Drayley Parking Ltd

**Document ID:** PRC-1911
**Dated:** 24 January 2025

Thornhurst Street car park: machines are emptied every Sunday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Hollstead Market Square branch.

Aldley Road car park: machines are emptied every Monday. Two staff count the cash, seal it in a numbered safe bag and pay it in the next morning at Hollstead Station Road branch.

Each site banks its own takings. Cash from the two sites is never combined.

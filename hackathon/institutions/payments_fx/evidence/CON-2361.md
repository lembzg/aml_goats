# Supply contract with Selwick Dried Fruits Co

**Document ID:** CON-2361
**Dated:** 31 July 2026

This supply agreement establishes that Selwick Dried Fruits Co, based in Erbil, will provide Oakhurst Furniture Imports Ltd with shipments of dried apricots, figs, and dates. Deliveries are scheduled to occur on a quarterly basis over a 12-month period. All pricing is set in US dollars, and the terms stipulate that payment is required upon shipment.
